The problem

Rwanda’s farms are small and varied. Planners need to see where yields lag, across districts, crops and seasons, before they can decide where to look closer. Survey data holds the answer, but raw microdata is hard to use.

The solution

AgriPulse turns the NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey into comparisons, a yield estimator and a transparent risk view, so a planner can go from a district to a short list of questions to investigate.

Key capabilities

Dashboard

Yield by crop, season and district, plus input associations.

Map

Filter districts by province, season and crop; click for details.

Yield prediction

Estimate yield from plot characteristics known before harvest.

Risk and priorities

Low, medium and high risk with the rule shown openly.

How the platform works

NISR data→Cleaning→Statistics→ML prediction→Risk assessment→Decision support
